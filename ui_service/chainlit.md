# Advanced Hybrid RAG

Ask questions about **your documents**, documents **shared** with you, your
**Google Drive** (admins) and the **web**. Every answer cites its sources and
is checked for groundedness before you see it.

**Tips**

- **Drop files** (PDF, DOCX, PNG, JPG) into the message box to add them:
  they are OCR'd with Docling and indexed, then you can ask about them.
- **Commands:** type `/` for `/docs`, `/web`, `/drive`, `/catalog`.
- **Settings** (⚙ in the composer): switch web / Drive search on or off,
  hide the pipeline steps, choose a concise or detailed answer style.
- **MCP:** connect your own MCP servers (plug icon) and their tools are used
  automatically when a question needs them.
- Click a citation like **[1]** to see the exact passage it came from.
